Lux-RC 371 PIN codes

A PIN is entered by switching the light on and off with the tail (clicky) switch. Lights with an electronic momentary button use button shortcuts instead.

Which board do you have?

371 v1

v1

The first-generation board, also sold as the 371D.

  • Up to 4 modes
  • Voltage meter (PIN 111)
  • Motion sensing on boards made from 2019
  • No memory files, double-click or battery test

371 v2

v2

The newer board, sold as the 371 v2.0. The 372 v2.0 uses the same PINs as the 371 v2.

  • 32 modes in 4 files of 8
  • Double-click action
  • Battery test
  • Standby with a locator beacon

How to enter a PIN

  1. Each digit is that many short on-flashes. Keep each flash under half a second, about 0.2 s. In a single-digit PIN, keep each flash to 0.25 s or less.
  2. Between digits leave the light off for about 1 s. Anything from 0.5 s to 1.25 s works; longer than 1.25 s wipes the digits you have entered.
  3. After the last digit wait 1 s, then switch the light on and leave it on.

Example: PIN 1-1-2 is one flash, pause, one flash, pause, two flashes, pause, then on.

Tip: point the light away from your eyes and count your clicks. Do not rely on seeing the flashes.

What does PIN 111 do on the Lux-RC 371 v1 and v2?

Voltage meter. Shows the battery voltage as flashes.

The light answers:
example: 3.6 V

White flashes count whole volts, red flashes count tenths. 3.6 V is 3 white then 6 red. A full cell is about 4.2 V and empty is below 3.0 V. With two cells it shows the average per cell.

What does PIN 113 do on the Lux-RC 371 v2 (v2 only)?

Battery test for one fully charged cell.

The light answers:
example: 4 of 5 stars

The light ramps up and then rates the battery from 1 to 5 stars. Fast red flashing means the test failed: charge the cell and try again.

What does PIN 311 do on the Lux-RC 371 v2 (v2 only)?

Shows the state of all 7 offline switches.

The light answers:
example: 7 switches

7 flashes, one per switch. Red means off, white means on.

4

v2 only

What does PIN 4 do on the Lux-RC 371 v2 (v2 only)?

Some vendors use 1 · 1 · 4.

Chooses the memory file.

The light answers:
files 1 to 4

It flashes the number of the current file, then steps through the 4 files. Switch the light off on the one you want.

Settings

Each PIN toggles one of the 7 offline switches. The light confirms with that many flashes: white means the switch is now on, red means it is now off. Enter the same PIN again to flip it back, and use PIN 311 to check all seven.
